1. Custom list rows keep their declared height

Two custom rows with Height: Flex(1) share a ten-line list. The row wrapper now exposes its latest rendered child's dimensions to the parent layout.

2. Modal backdrop changes repaint the entire screen

Both captures below follow the same Ctrl+B action: change an open modal's backdrop from 50% black to 50% red. Backdrop changes now trigger a full repaint because their effect extends beyond the modal's bounds.

Checked: the minimal 20×10 reproduction went from 195 mismatched cells to zero against an independent full render. Tests also cover transparent and theme-default backdrops, and a nested overlay.
